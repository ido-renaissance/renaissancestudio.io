Questions

The things people ask when they call. Anything else, just ring the shop.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome any time we're open. Appointments get priority, so if you're coming Thursday or Friday after 5, booking saves you a wait.

What's the difference between a skin fade and a regular fade?

A skin fade takes the sides down to bare skin at the bottom before blending up. A regular taper or fade stops at a short guard. Not sure which you want? Bring a photo and we'll talk it through before the clippers come out. Same price either way.

What happens if I don't like my cut?

Tell us before you leave and we'll fix it right there. If you notice something at home, come back within 7 days and we'll clean it up for free.

Can I bring my kids?

Please do. Kids' cuts are $25 for 12 and under. A parent or guardian stays in the shop. Jaz is our go-to for little ones who aren't sure about clippers yet.

What's your cancellation policy?

Text or call at least 2 hours ahead so we can give the chair to a walk-in. We're not charging no-show fees right now. We'd just appreciate the heads-up.

Where do I park?

Street parking on Park Street right out front, plus spots on the side streets. Several bus routes stop within a block.
