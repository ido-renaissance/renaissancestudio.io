Hail or wind come through? Here's what to do first.

Spring hail and late-summer storms are part of life in Charlotte. If you think your roof took a hit, a calm, documented approach gets claims paid. We help with the paperwork and the adjuster meeting at no charge.

Your first 48 hours

  1. Stay off the roof. Wet shingles are slick, and damage is easier to spot from a ladder with the right eye.
  2. Photograph from the ground: dented gutters, downspouts, AC fins and shingle granules in the gutters.
  3. Stop active leaks with a bucket and towels. Call us for an emergency tarp.
  4. Call your insurer to open a claim and write down the claim number.

What we do for you

  • Emergency tarping, usually within 24 hours ($295–$650, often reimbursed by your policy)
  • A dated damage report with test-square photos your adjuster can use
  • Meet your adjuster on the roof so nothing gets missed
  • Review the insurance scope line by line and flag missing items such as drip edge, code upgrades and decking

Questions people ask us

Don't see yours? Call (704) 555-0117. You'll get Marcus, not a call center.

Is the inspection really free? What's the catch?

It's free, with no obligation and no follow-up calls unless you ask for them. It's how a new company earns work: you see how we operate before you spend a dollar. You keep the photo report either way.

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

Age matters. Most 3-tab roofs in our climate are near the end at 15 to 20 years. But it's mostly about what we find: widespread granule loss, brittle or curling shingles, and soft decking point to replacement. A single leak at a pipe boot or chimney is usually a repair. We'll tell you which, with photos.

You're a brand-new company. Why should I trust you with my roof?

Fair question. We can't point to years of history, so we lean on things you can verify: a state license, insurance certificates sent to you directly, permits on file, a deposit capped at 30%, and a written workmanship warranty. Ask for any of it before you sign.

Do I need to be home on install day?

Not for the whole day. We'd like to see you at the start to confirm colors and access, and at the end for the walkthrough. Please move cars out of the driveway and take anything fragile off interior walls. Hammering can shake pictures loose.

How long does a roof replacement take, and what about rain?

Most shingle roofs take one to two days; metal takes four to seven. We watch the forecast closely and only open up what we can dry-in the same day. If weather moves the date, we call you the evening before.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, through a third-party lender, with options including 12 months same-as-cash for approved buyers. We'll go over terms in person. There's no pressure to use it, and it never changes your price.

What areas do you serve?

Homes within about 30 miles of Charlotte, including Matthews, Mint Hill, Huntersville, Cornelius, Pineville, Concord and Mooresville. Further out? Call and ask.
