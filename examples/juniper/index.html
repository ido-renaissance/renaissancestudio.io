Your first visit, step by step.

Plan on about 90 minutes. If it's been a long time, or you're nervous, tell us when you book. We'll slow everything down and won't comment on how long it's been.

  1. Paperwork at home

    We text you a short health form the day you book. Five minutes on your phone, so you're not juggling a clipboard in the lobby.

  2. Settle in

    Grab tea or water, and a weighted blanket if you'd like one. Headphones are on the counter for the chair.

  3. Talk first, tools later

    Dr. Raman sits down with you, chair upright, to hear what's bothering you and what you hope for.

  4. Exam & low-dose x-rays

    Digital x-rays and intraoral photos you can see on the screen, so you're looking at the same thing we are.

  5. A gentle cleaning

    Unless you need a deeper cleaning first, we do it the same day. We'll say so plainly if that changes.

  6. Your plan, in writing

    You leave with a printed and emailed plan: what's needed, what's optional, and the cost after insurance.

Questions people ask us.

Something not covered here? Call us. A real person at the front desk will answer.

I haven't been to a dentist in years. Will I get a lecture?

No. Plenty of people take a break from the dentist, for all kinds of reasons. We'll look at where things are today, explain your options without judgment and help you start wherever you're comfortable.

What age should my child have their first visit?

By their first birthday, or within six months of their first tooth. Those early visits are short and mostly about getting comfortable: a ride in the chair, a quick look and tips for brushing at home.

Do you offer anything for dental anxiety?

Yes. We have noise-cancelling headphones, weighted blankets, a hand signal to pause at any time, and nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for $55 per visit. You can also book a no-treatment "meet and talk" appointment at no charge.

How does the $99 new-patient offer work?

It's for our first 150 new patients without dental insurance and covers a comprehensive exam, full x-rays and a routine cleaning. If you need a deeper periodontal cleaning instead, we'll explain why and quote it before going ahead.

What if I have a dental emergency after hours?

Existing patients can call our main number after hours and follow the prompt to reach Dr. Raman's on-call line. For serious facial swelling, trouble breathing or swallowing, or a head injury, go to the nearest emergency room.

Is the office accessible?

Our suite is on the ground floor with a step-free entrance, wide doorways and an accessible restroom. Our treatment chairs lower for easier transfers. Let us know about any needs when you book and we'll plan around them.
