A note from Marisol

I learned to bake standing on a chair in my abuela's kitchen, arguing about how thick the concha topping should be. (Thicker. I won.) For a long time I baked for other people's kitchens and dreamed about a little shop of my own.

This is it. Six tables, a long glass case, and a door that opens before most of San Antonio is awake. Come say hi. I'm usually the one with flour on my glasses.

— Marisol Treviño

How a morning goes at Marisol Bakehouse

  1. 3:45
    The dough comes out

    Concha dough is mixed the afternoon before and rests overnight in the cooler. That slow rise is where the flavor comes from.

  2. 4:30
    Shaping and topping

    Every bun is rounded by hand and pressed with the shell pattern. Orejas get laminated and rolled in sugar.

  3. 6:30
    Doors open, first trays out

    The café de olla has been simmering for an hour. The first conchas are still too warm to hold.

  4. 2:00
    Whatever's left goes out the door

    We don't sell yesterday's bread. Anything left at close is donated that afternoon to a neighborhood pantry.

Questions people ask at the counter

Do you use lard?

Our conchas, cuernitos and orejas are made with butter. The polvorones and marranitos use a little manteca, the traditional way, and they're labeled on the case. If you need something lard-free, ask and we'll point you to it.

Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

Not yet, and I'd rather be honest than sell you something from a shared-flour kitchen and call it safe. Our café drinks can be made with oat or almond milk, and the champurrado is dairy-free on request.

Can I order a custom tres leches?

Yes. Rounds and half sheets, with fresa or cajeta on top and writing for free. Give us 48 hours, and for anything bigger than a half sheet please call so we can talk it through.

When is pan de muerto available?

From mid-October through November 2. Pre-orders open October 1 and the family-size loaves do sell out the last weekend, so order early if it's for your ofrenda.

Do you deliver?

For catering orders of three dozen or more, we deliver anywhere inside Loop 410 for $15. Everyday orders are pickup only for now, while we're a small team.

Is there seating?

Six small tables inside and a couple out front under the awning. Laptops are welcome on weekdays; on Saturday mornings we ask you to share.
