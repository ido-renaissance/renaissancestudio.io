Questions people actually ask us

Something not covered here? Call and ask. You'll get a real answer, not a sales pitch.

Is "same-day" really same-day?

For calls before 2 p.m. on weekdays, yes. After that, or on a fully booked day, we'll give you the first slot the next morning and an honest arrival window. If you have no heat and it's below freezing, call any time: we run an after-hours line for exactly that.

What does the $89 diagnostic cover?

The trip, a full inspection of the system, and a written explanation of what's wrong with a flat-rate price to fix it. If you go ahead with the repair, the $89 comes off the bill. If you don't, you still keep the report.

Do heat pumps actually work in a Denver winter?

Current cold-climate models do. Many deliver full heat down to around 5°F and keep working well below zero. For older or drafty homes we often recommend a dual-fuel setup, where a gas furnace takes over on the coldest nights. We'll run the numbers for your house before recommending anything.

Does altitude matter for my furnace?

It does. Gas appliances at 5,280 ft need their gas pressure and combustion set for thinner air. A furnace set up for sea level can run hot, sooty or inefficient. We check and adjust this on every tune-up and install.

Which brands do you work on?

We repair all the major residential brands. For installs we're not locked to one manufacturer, so we recommend what fits your house and budget and explain why.

Can I cancel the Care plan?

Anytime, with a phone call or email. There's no cancellation fee and no minimum term. If you've already had a tune-up that month, there's nothing to pay back.
