That black film on Florida concrete isn't dirt you can sweep. It's mold, algae and tannin stain baked in by sun and summer rain. Here's how we get it out without etching the surface.

Straight answers

Questions I get asked at almost every quote. Don't see yours? Call (239) 555-0148.

What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high pressure and is right for hard surfaces like concrete and pavers. Soft washing uses about the pressure of a garden hose plus a cleaning solution that kills the mold and algae at the root. It's what we use on roofs, stucco, screens and anything that can be damaged by force.

Will you damage my tile roof?

No one walks on your tile. We work from ladders and the ground with low pressure, so there's nothing to crack and no granules or coating stripped off. Most roof cleanings take a few hours, and the streaks keep fading for about a week after.

Is it safe for my plants, pool and pets?

We wet your plants before we start, keep runoff away from the pool, and rinse everything down again when we're done. Please keep pets inside until surfaces are dry, usually about an hour.

Do I need to be home?

Not for exterior work. We need an outdoor water spigot that works and access to the areas we're cleaning (gate code, unlocked lanai door). We'll text you before we arrive and send photos when we finish.

How long does it stay clean in Southwest Florida?

Honestly, it depends on shade, sprinklers and how close you are to water. Most driveways look good for 12 to 18 months, roofs usually two to three years. We post-treat to slow regrowth, and we'll tell you when it's actually worth calling us back.

What if it rains on the day?

Light rain doesn't affect a wash. For roofs and storms we reschedule at no charge and text you the new time. Summer afternoons, we try to book you in the morning.

How do I pay?

After the job, once you've had a look. We take card, Zelle, check or cash. No deposits on residential work.
