Common questions

Is the design really free?

Yes. We design your homepage first and send it to you. If you don't like it, you don't pay anything and there's nothing to cancel.

I'm not good with computers. Is that a problem?

Not at all. You answer a few simple questions and we do the rest: design, setup and getting it online. Everything happens by email or text. No calls needed.

Do I have to pay every month?

Nothing monthly from us. Hosting is your own Hostinger account, a few dollars a month, with your domain free the first year.

How fast is it?

After you approve your design and send us your details, we send the first version of your full site within 24 hours.

Who owns the website?

You do. The website, the domain and the hosting account are all yours. You can change the text and photos yourself any time.

Can you build it on Wix or Squarespace?

No. We build on WordPress, which you own and control. We check this with you before you pay.
