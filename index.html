Before you ask.

All pricing questions →

What if the concept isn't for me?

Don't buy it. The concept is free. You only pay for the build after you approve the direction.

I only have a name. Is that enough?

For a first direction, yes. Tell us the industry and city too. When it's time to build, we'll ask for your contact details, hours and what sets you apart. If you don't have photos, we can use ours.

Can you publish into my site builder?

We build on WordPress or deliver files. We can't publish into Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, GoDaddy builder or Webflow. We check before checkout so you don't pay for a site you can't use.

What about bookings, a shop or more pages?

Those aren't part of the standard build. Neither are ongoing marketing or SEO retainers. Ask us about what you need.

Who do I talk to?

James manages the project. Email james@renaissancestudio.io. No calls needed.
