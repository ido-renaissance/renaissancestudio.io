The fine print,
in plain type.

Something specific to your setup? Email James, our project manager:
james@renaissancestudio.io.

What if I don't like the free concept?

Pass on it. The concept isn't an order. Don't pay until you like the direction and know where the site will live.

Is there a monthly payment to the studio?

No. One payment of $799 covers the build. No monthly studio fee, discounts or payment plans.

How much does hosting cost?

We recommend Hostinger, typically a few dollars a month. The exact bill depends on the plan and term you choose. Pay the host directly; you don't rent the site from us.

Can I use a domain I already own?

Yes. Keep the domain in your own account. We handle the connection. If you need a new domain, Hostinger includes one free the first year; renewals are separate.

When will I see the first version?

Within 24 hours after you send the completed intake form. That's the first version for review, not a deadline to launch without your approval.

How do revisions work?

Send the changes you'd like in one set of notes. We update the page and you review it again. Up to two rounds are included before launch.

Can you deliver into my site builder?

Not into Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, GoDaddy builder or Webflow. We check before payment. Choose WordPress hosting or files instead. If the builder is a must, please don't order.

What if I already have WordPress hosting?

Use that account. Share access by invitation so we can set up the site. Don't email a password.

Who owns what after launch?

You own the site, the domain and the hosting account. You manage renewals with your providers; you don't need an ongoing studio agreement to keep the site.

Can I get a refund before work starts?

Email James as soon as you know you want to cancel, before sending the build form. Ask for a refund and include the business name so he can find the order and check whether work has started. If refund eligibility matters to your decision, confirm it before paying.

What do you need from me?

About ten minutes for a form: your contact details, hours, story, logo and photos if you have them, requested changes and hosting choice. Use our images if you don't have photos. No call needed.

Can I edit the site myself later?

Yes. With the WordPress build, you can change text and photos yourself. If you select files only, you're choosing to manage deployment yourself.

Does the price include bookings or a shop?

No. Online stores, booking systems, extra pages and ongoing marketing or SEO work are outside the one-page build. Ask us about your requirements.
