Where your details go.

A concept needs a brief. A build needs content. Neither needs your password.

Previewing a direction

The sample viewer loads a page from our examples library. Your business name and city can appear in its URL to personalize the layout. Use public business details, not confidential information. The studio pages request typefaces from Google Fonts; like other web requests, those requests include connection information such as an IP address.

Requesting a concept or contact

We use the business, industry, description, location, name, contact details and message you provide to respond to your request. When you continue to checkout, the hosting choice travels with the brief. The submission also includes the page and any campaign source, medium and campaign name carried from your landing link.

When the submission service is connected, the forms send that information to the studio's intake service. In preview mode they save it in this browser instead. A preview submission isn't a delivered message.

Payment and project files

Live checkout uses Stripe. The checkout link includes a business reference and, if you provided one, your email to prefill checkout. The local preview doesn't collect a card or process a charge.

The project form collects the public details, story, images, links, changes and hosting choice needed to build your site. With the order service connected, confirming sends those fields and files to that service. Otherwise the completed form stays in your browser. Never include customer records, card details or passwords.

On this device

Local storage keeps recent submissions, order details and the current intake draft, including image previews. Session storage carries campaign information between pages in the same browsing session. Someone with access to the same browser profile may be able to see saved details.

To remove device copies, clear this site's data in your browser settings. This also removes unfinished drafts and doesn't retract anything already sent. Use your own device for a real project intake.

Questions or corrections

Email James at james@renaissancestudio.io to ask about, correct or request removal of information you've sent. Include the business name so he can identify the project.
