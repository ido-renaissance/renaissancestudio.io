The build sequence.

The first thing we ask you to look at is a homepage, not an invoice. Here is where each decision happens.

Project timingRS / Process

Two clocks.

Concept design
About 2 minutes
Client intake
About 10 minutes
First version
Within 24 hours of intake

Concept time is the time to put a direction together. Build time starts when we have your completed form, not when you open checkout.

  1. You reply.

    We look for businesses that registered days ago. If we wrote to you, reply to the email. Found us first? Tell us about your business instead.

  2. See the free concept.

    Our team and AI design system turn the brief into a homepage direction. The live preview lets you try a sample layout immediately. Your own concept is designed around your business.

  3. Decide if it fits.

    Look at the type, colors and order of the page. Would you want this to introduce your business? If not, there is no charge and nothing to cancel.

  4. Choose a home. Pay once.

    Before payment, choose new hosting, existing WordPress hosting or files. If you need us to publish into a site builder, stop here. If the setup fits and you approve the direction, the full build is $799.

  5. Send the real details.

    One short form collects your hours, contact details, logo, photos, what makes you different and the changes you want. No photos yet? We can supply them. No call to book.

  6. Check the first version.

    Within 24 hours of the completed form, we send your one-page site for review. Read the services, check the hours, and try the contact section on your phone.

  7. Mark it up.

    You have up to two rounds of revisions before launch. Send one collected set of notes per round so we can work through the page together by email.

  8. Connect the domain.

    The hosting account is yours. We recommend Hostinger: a few dollars a month, with a domain free the first year. Invite us to the account. We set up the site and connect your domain. Chose files? We hand them over rather than publishing.

  9. It's yours.

    You own the finished site, domain and hosting account. With WordPress, you can change text and photos without asking us. The hosting bill goes to you. There is no monthly bill from the studio.
