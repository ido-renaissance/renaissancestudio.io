Build terms.

What you approve, what we deliver and who holds the keys.

Concept first

Viewing sample sites or requesting a concept doesn't commit you to a purchase. The interactive samples represent fictional businesses. A personalized sample is a first direction, not a launched business website.

The order

Approve the direction and choose a supported delivery method before paying. The $799 one-time fee covers a complete one-page website with hero, services, about, FAQ and contact sections. There is no monthly studio fee. The clearly marked demo checkout doesn't charge you or place a live order.

Content and review

Supply accurate contact details, hours and business information. Only supply text, logos and images you have permission to publish. We use these to build the site. Check the details before approving launch.

We send the first version within 24 hours of receiving the completed intake form. Up to two revision rounds are included before launch. Extra pages, online stores, booking systems and ongoing marketing or SEO retainers aren't included. Ask us before relying on anything outside this scope.

Hosting and ownership

You own the finished site, domain and hosting account. You pay your providers directly and handle their renewals. For the WordPress build, share access by invitation and we handle setup and domain connection. Files-only delivery leaves installation to you.

We can't publish into Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, GoDaddy builder or Webflow. If delivery into a builder is essential, don't proceed to payment.

Order changes or cancellation

If you need to stop an order or request a refund before work starts, email James promptly with the business name. Don't wait for the first version. Ask about any refund conditions you need clarified before paying.

Write to james@renaissancestudio.io. For the complete scope, see pricing and FAQ.
